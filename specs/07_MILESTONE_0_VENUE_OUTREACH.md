# Milestone 0: Venue Outreach Protocol & Script

## 1. Context & Timing
- **Target Interviewee:** General Manager or Head Sommelier.
- **Timing Window:** Tuesday through Thursday between 2:30 PM and 4:30 PM (the afternoon service lull). Never approach during prep (before 12 PM) or evening service (after 6 PM).
- **Core Principle:** Respect their time, acknowledge the cost of goods, avoid false promises, and do not sound like a SaaS salesperson.

---

## 2. The 3-Minute In-Person Script

> **Intro:**  
> "Hi [Name / Manager], do you have two minutes? I’m not selling an ad or a POS system. I live locally in Bermondsey and I’m putting together a small, tightly curated food and wine guide called London Curated."  
>  
> *(Pause for acknowledgement)*  
>  
> **Editorial Question (Builds Rapport):**  
> "We’re focusing strictly on 5 independent neighborhood spots on and around Bermondsey Street, including [Venue Name]. We don't do star ratings or generic reviews. Instead, we highlight one marquee dish alongside a thoughtful wine pairing.  
>  
> First, I’d love to make sure our tasting note aligns with what your sommelier actually recommends. For your [Dish Name], what’s the bottle or glass you love pouring with it right now?"  
>  
> *(Listen and note down their recommendation)*  
>  
> **Pilot Perk Proposal (Low Stakes & Honest):**  
> "Second question—and feel free to say no: We’re testing a small, invite-only dining club for local Bermondsey food and wine lovers. The goal is to drive diners in on your slower shifts—specifically Tuesday or Wednesday evenings.  
>  
> We want to test a simple pilot: if we send a table of two in on a quiet midweek night, would you be open to offering them a small perk—like a complimentary tasting pour of that pairing or an aperitif—on a short two-week trial? Zero listing fee, no contract. We just want to see if the incentive actually fills empty mid-week covers for you."  
>  
> *(Listen carefully to their reaction)*  
>  
> - **If positive:** "Brilliant. May I grab your email so I can send a two-line confirmation before we launch the pilot?"  
> - **If cost/margin concern:** "Completely understand—cost of goods is real. Even an off-menu bite from the kitchen works. Let me leave you my card and send a one-page summary."  
> - **If busy / 'Send info':** "Understood. What's the best direct email to send a 30-second summary?"

---

## 3. The 2-Hour Follow-Up Message

**Subject:** London Curated × [Venue Name] — quick follow-up

```text
Hi [Name],

Thanks for the quick chat earlier during the lull at [Venue Name].

As mentioned, London Curated is an independent, editorial guide launching with just 5 Bermondsey dining rooms (José, Casse-Croûte, Baccalà, Le Chevalier, and Pique-Nique). 

Our goal is simple: connect local wine and food enthusiasts with the best independent tables in the neighborhood, with a strong focus on what to eat and what to drink with it.

The pilot we'd love to test with you:
1. The Feature: An editorial spotlight on your [Dish Name] with your recommended wine pairing ([Wine Name]).
2. The Midweek Trial: A two-week test where our local waitlist members receive a small perk (e.g. a small tasting pour of the paired wine) exclusively on quiet Tuesday/Wednesday shifts with advance booking.
3. Zero Fees: No listing fees, no commissions on the food, and no long-term commitment. We simply want to test whether this drives meaningful midweek spend.

I've attached a preview of how [Venue Name]'s profile will look. Let me know if the wine pairing details look good to you, and whether you'd be open to a 2-week trial next month.

Warmly,

[Your Name]
London Curated
[Your Phone Number]
london-curated.com
```
