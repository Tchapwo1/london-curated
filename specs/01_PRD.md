# 📋 Pillar 1: Product Requirements Document (PRD) — London Curated

## 1. Executive Summary & Value Proposition
**London Curated** is an exclusive, luxury-editorial culinary discovery platform and digital sommelier index for London's finest dining neighborhoods, beginning with **Bermondsey & London Bridge**. 

Instead of an uncurated, cluttered directory that begs restaurant owners for listing fees, London Curated operates as an authoritative gastronomic guide: pairing each venue's signature dish with an intelligent sommelier wine recommendation, connecting directly to luxury wine merchants (Berry Bros. & Rudd, Majestic, Vivino) for high-margin affiliate revenue, and offering diners a curated "Foodie Passport" membership.

---

## 2. Target Persona & Core Problem
- **Primary Persona:** Affluent London professionals, food connoisseurs, corporate expense-account diners, and high-end cultural tourists seeking vetted, Michelin-grade dining experiences without wading through spammy Yelp/TripAdvisor reviews.
- **The Core Problem with Legacy Directories:**
  - Standard directories are ugly, cluttered, and unreliable.
  - Restaurant managers refuse to pay £50/month for directory ads because the ROI is unproven.
  - Diners lack actionable, high-conviction guidance: *"What is the single best dish to order here, and what wine should I drink with it?"*
- **Our Value Promise:**
  - Zero clutter: Only vetted, exceptional dining destinations (starting with 21 Bermondsey icons including Trivet, Story, José, Casse-Croûte).
  - The Sommelier Edge: Every signature dish is paired with a specific wine, regional rationale, and 1-click buying link.

---

## 3. The 3-Tier Revenue Architecture
1. **Tier 1: Sommelier Wine E-Commerce Affiliate (Immediate Cashflow)**
   - Every restaurant profile spotlights its marquee dish alongside an exact wine pairing generated by the `sommelier-pairing` engine.
   - 1-click "Order This Bottle" buttons link to Berry Bros. & Rudd, Majestic, and Vivino, earning **8%–12% commission** per transaction (£5–£25 per bottle).
2. **Tier 2: The Bermondsey Dining Club Passport (B2C Membership)**
   - A digital membership pass (£19–£29 one-time or £9/month) unlocking off-menu perks (e.g. complimentary glass of sommelier-paired wine, priority reservations, secret dessert) at participating venues.
3. **Tier 3: Seasonal Hospitality Spotlight Drops (B2B Editorial)**
   - Curated seasonal drops (£350–£750 per campaign) for boutique restaurant groups (e.g., José Pizarro Group, Trivet, Story) to feature seasonal tasting menus.

---

## 4. Scope & Core Features (v1.0)
- **Feature 1: The 21 Anchor Venues of Bermondsey:** Enriched profiles with high-resolution photography, head chef bios, and verified 5/5 Food Hygiene badges.
- **Feature 2: Interactive Spatial Map (MapLibre GL):** Fluid, vector-tiled map with custom obsidian/champagne pins, walking distances, and neighborhood clustering.
- **Feature 3: The Sommelier Pairing Drawer:** Dynamic pairing cards detailing wine category, appellation, biochemical rationale (tannin/fat/acidity balance), and benchmark bottle links.
- **Feature 4: Dietary & Atmosphere Filter:** Seamless filtering by vibe (Romantic, Business, Casual Tapas, Michelin Star) and dietary preference (Seafood, Beef, Vegetarian, Gluten-Free).
- **Feature 5: 1-Click Booking Integration:** Direct deep-links to each venue's official booking engine (OpenTable, SevenRooms, Resy).

---

## 5. Out-of-Scope (v1.0 Non-Goals)
- Handling direct payment processing for restaurant table reservations (we deep-link to SevenRooms/OpenTable).
- In-house food delivery or courier logistics (UberEats/Deliveroo integration).
- Crowd-sourced open reviews (curation is editorial and authoritative).
