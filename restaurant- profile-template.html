The Curated Review

The Vibe: Stepping into Dishoom Soho is like walking onto a film set of a 1960s Bombay café. It is perpetually buzzing, dimly lit, and smells intoxicatingly of incense and roasting spices. Expect a queue outside, but the complimentary chai makes the wait entirely palatable.

The Food: While the breakfast menu (specifically the iconic bacon naan roll) rightly gets all the press, the evening menu is a masterclass in comfort. The House Black Daal, simmered for 24 hours, is legendary. Pair it with the Ruby Murray and a bowl of gunpowder potatoes.

The Verdict: Perfect for high-energy group dinners, impressive dates, or a decadent weekend brunch. It rarely misses the mark.
